Gromov-Wasserstein Distance

The Gromov-Wasserstein distance is motivated by

  • Comparing objects that lie in different metric spaces (e.g. 2D vs 3D, images vs graphs);
  • Embedding isometric properties, including translation invariance, rotation invariance, reflection invariance, even when the objects lie in the same metric space.
