Introduction

We all use a camera to take pictures. A camera is an imaging device that uses visible light. When you go to a hospital, you may use X-ray imaging to see your bones, or use MRI to see your soft tissues, or use ultrasound to see your internal organs.

More generally, many different kinds of waves can be used to "take a picture". This includes different wavelengths of electromagnetic waves (light, X-ray, radio, etc.) and mechanical waves (sound, ultrasound, etc.).
